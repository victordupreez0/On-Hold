How it works

From “this feels off” to a plain answer.

  1. 1

    Paste it, show it or say it

    Paste the message, share a screenshot, or tap Speak and describe it. Someone on the phone right now? Call mode shows the safety basics straight away.

    Paste Screenshot Speak Call

  2. 2

    Take a breath

    A calm pause, at least three seconds, while On Hold checks. Scammers rush you. On Hold slows you down.

  3. 3

    Get a plain verdict

    Stop, Wait or Looks okay, with the reasons and exactly what to do now. On a call, it gives you the words to say before you hang up.

  4. 4

    Family hears, only if you said yes

    Link a son, daughter or friend and choose what they're told. On a Stop they get the warning, never your messages.

  • Stop

    It matches a known scam. Don't reply, pay or tap any link.

  • Wait

    Something isn't right, but it's not certain. Check with someone first.

  • Looks okay

    Nothing worrying found. On Hold never says something is certainly safe.

Privacy

What you check is never stored.

Scam messages often contain your own account details. On Hold reads the message or screenshot, gives you a verdict, and keeps only the verdict. Not the message. Not the picture.

  • Never kept

    Only the verdict is saved, so your history can show it.

  • Your family, your call

    They see a warning, never your messages, and only if you agreed.

  • Suburbs, not addresses

    The scam map never shows where anyone lives.
