Likely questions, and what to open

QuestionShow
Where is the AI, and why is it core?backend/app/ai.py: SYSTEM_PROMPT, ScamVerdict, run_check. Every check goes through it; voice goes through Whisper first (transcribe).
How do you stop prompt injection?The <message> tags and the rule in SYSTEM_PROMPT: an instruction inside a message is itself a scam signal.
How do you know it’s accurate?backend/scripts/eval.py: 29 labelled messages, 29/29 on Sonnet 5 and Haiku 5.5, no dangerous misses. Results in docs/testing.md.
Can the AI invent a link?No: SOURCES and SourceId in ai.py; the schema only allows those 8 ids.
Why Haiku and not a bigger model?Same score on the test, about 16× cheaper (R0.005 a check), a bit faster. /health reports the live model.
How is data secured?backend/app/auth.py (Supabase JWT checked against its keys); queries scoped to the user in main.py (e.g. get_check); RLS on with no policies in supabase/migrations.
Rate limiting and cost?_check_rate_limit in main.py: 30 AI calls an hour per person, with an advisory lock. Prompt caching in run_check.
How does family consent work?create_check in main.py: a Stop alerts only links with share_verdicts or share_calls on.
Show the CRUD.Reports and admin routes in main.py; all 48 listed in backend/docs/api.md.
Deployment?Vercel: backend/vercel.json (Frankfurt) and the transaction pooler (backend/app/db.py). Cloudflare Pages: website/README.md.
Version control and process?GitHub network graph: feature → dev → main with merge commits; CI in .github/workflows/ci.yml.
What went wrong along the way?The bug log in docs/testing.md (42 entries), e.g. the live API hanging until it moved to the transaction pooler.
